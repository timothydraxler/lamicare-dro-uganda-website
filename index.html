Who We Are

Lamicare DRO Uganda

LAMICARE DRO Uganda is a non-governmental, non-profit, and community-based organization operating in Uganda, born from a collective vision of a society where every person with a disability is recognized, valued, and empowered to live with dignity and full participation.

We are a community-driven organization, devoted to building a future free from discrimination and brimming with opportunity for persons with disabilities. Our work is guided by our core values of dignity, inclusion, accountability, courage, and justice.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

MISSION

Upholding Dignity

To uphold the dignity of persons with disabilities through robust advocacy and awareness campaigns, challenging stigma and promoting a society where their rights are respected, and they can live with full dignity and influence.

VISION

Empowered Communities

A society where persons with disabilities are empowered to fully exercise their rights, live with dignity, and actively lead, thrive, and shape their communities.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

D

Dignity

Recognizing the inherent value of every person with a disability, ensuring they are treated with respect, honour, and autonomy.

I

Inclusion

Creating environments and systems where persons with disabilities fully participate in all aspects of society.

A

Accountability

Holding ourselves and others responsible for upholding the rights and dignity of persons with disabilities.

C

Courage

Standing up for the rights and dignity of persons with disabilities, even when it is difficult or unpopular.

J

Justice

Working towards equitable access to resources, opportunities, and remedies for rights violations.

How We Work

Our Approach

Lamicare DRO Uganda is deeply rooted in multisectoral community and humanitarian service. We believe that true change comes from centring the voices and leadership of persons with disabilities.

Leadership by Experience

Our membership structure includes significant representation of persons with disabilities on our Board of Directors, reflecting our commitment to authentic leadership.

Transparency & Accountability

We operate with rigorous financial management subject to independent audits, zero tolerance for corruption and fraud, and robust policies on conflicts of interest and data privacy.

Community-Driven

We partner with grassroots advocates, professional experts, critical friends, mission-aligned donors, and media allies to amplify impact and sustain change.
