What We Do

Transformative action that empowers persons with disabilities and promotes an inclusive society.

1. Advocacy and Policy Reform

We actively advocate for legal reforms and the full realization of the rights of persons with disabilities, working to embed inclusive policies, processes, and systems across all sectors.

Our advocacy challenges stigma, raises awareness, and pushes for systemic change so that the rights of persons with disabilities are respected in law and in practice.

2. Leadership and Empowerment

We empower persons with disabilities through targeted leadership and mentorship training programs, enhancing their skills, participation, and influence within their communities and beyond.

By investing in leadership from within the disability community, we build the capacity for long-term, sustainable change led by those with lived experience.

3. Inclusive Education and Sustainable Livelihoods

We develop, implement, and support inclusive education and sustainable livelihood programs that create equal opportunities, promote independence, and enhance the socio-economic well-being of persons with disabilities.

From accessible learning environments to skills training and economic empowerment, we work so that every person can thrive.

4. Promoting Civic Participation and Social Change

We facilitate platforms that enhance meaningful civic participation, dialogue, and collective action, driving social change and enabling a truly inclusive society.

Persons with disabilities must not only be present — they must lead, shape decisions, and influence the communities they live in.

5. Building Strategic Partnerships

LAMICARE DRO Uganda builds strong partnerships with government, civil society organizations, international partners, and other stakeholders to collectively advance the rights and inclusion of persons with disabilities.

Our impact is amplified through strategic partnerships with communities, grassroots organizations, and governments, as well as the generous support of individuals and organizations that share our commitment.

Our Valued Partners

Who Makes Our Work Possible

Disabled Leaders – Grassroots Advocates

Individuals with lived experience who are powerful grassroots activists, community organizers, and truth-tellers. They provide invaluable credibility, urgency, and a direct connection to the realities faced by persons with disabilities.

Disabled Leaders – Professional Experts

Professionals with disabilities who are experts in law, education, social work and other relevant sectors. They bring institutional knowledge, challenge existing policies, and drive systemic change.

Critical Friends & Strategic Advisors

Individuals who offer constructive critique, challenge assumptions, and encourage rigorous analysis to ensure our strategies are robust and effective.

Mission-Aligned Donors & Mentors

Funders and mentors who deeply understand and champion a rights-based approach to disability inclusion, moving beyond traditional charity models.

Communications & Media Allies

Journalists, influencers, storytellers, and media professionals who effectively communicate our message and amplify the voices of persons with disabilities.
