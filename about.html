About Us

Born from a collective vision of a society where every person with a disability is recognized, valued, and empowered.

Who We Are

LAMICARE DRO Uganda is a non-governmental, non-profit, and community-based organization operating in Uganda. We were born from a collective vision of a society where every person with a disability is recognized, valued, and empowered to live with dignity and full participation.

We are a community-driven organization, devoted to building a future free from discrimination and brimming with opportunity for persons with disabilities. Our work is guided by our core values of dignity, inclusion, accountability, courage, and justice.

Community-Rooted Leadership

Our membership structure includes a significant representation of persons with disabilities on our Board of Directors. This reflects our deep commitment to centring the voices and leadership of those we serve.

We operate with transparency and accountability, ensuring that our financial management is rigorous and subject to independent audits. We maintain zero tolerance for corruption and fraud, with robust policies to manage conflicts of interest and ensure data privacy.

Our Mission

To uphold the dignity of persons with disabilities through robust advocacy and awareness campaigns, challenging stigma and promoting a society where their rights are respected, and they can live with full dignity and influence.

Our Vision

A society where persons with disabilities are empowered to fully exercise their rights, live with dignity, and actively lead, thrive, and shape their communities.

Our Core Values

D

Dignity

Recognizing the inherent value of every person with a disability, ensuring they are treated with respect, honour, and autonomy in all aspects of life.

I

Inclusion

Create environments, systems, and communities where persons with disabilities are fully integrated and participate meaningfully in all aspects of society—social, economic, political, and cultural.

A

Accountability

Holding ourselves and others responsible for upholding the rights and dignity of persons with disabilities.

C

Courage

Our commitment to stand up for the rights and dignity of persons with disabilities, even when it is difficult or unpopular.

J

Justice

We work towards a society where persons with disabilities have equitable access to resources, opportunities, and remedies for rights violations.

Our Approach

LAMICARE DRO Uganda is deeply rooted in multisectoral community and humanitarian service. We believe that true change comes from centring the voices and leadership of persons with disabilities.

We partner with and value:

  • Disabled Leaders – Grassroots Advocates: Individuals with lived experience who provide credibility, urgency, and direct connection to the realities faced by persons with disabilities.
  • Disabled Leaders – Professional Experts: Professionals with disabilities in law, education, social work and other fields who bring institutional knowledge and drive systemic change.
  • Critical Friends & Strategic Advisors: Individuals who offer constructive critique and challenge assumptions to keep our strategies robust.
  • Mission-Aligned Donors & Mentors: Funders and mentors who champion a rights-based approach beyond traditional charity models.
  • Communications & Media Allies: Journalists, influencers and storytellers who amplify the voices of persons with disabilities.
