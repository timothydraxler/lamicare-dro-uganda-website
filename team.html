Meet Our Team

Led by people with lived experience and committed professionals who centre the voices of persons with disabilities.

Our Leadership Commitment

Lamicare DRO Uganda is deeply rooted in the principle that true change comes from centring the voices and leadership of persons with disabilities. Our membership structure includes a significant representation of persons with disabilities on our Board of Directors — reflecting this commitment in practice, not just in policy.

We operate with transparency and accountability. Our financial management is rigorous and subject to independent audits. We maintain zero tolerance for corruption and fraud, with robust policies to manage conflicts of interest and ensure data privacy.

Governance & Operations

Board & Key Roles

Detailed profiles of Board members and staff will be published here as they are confirmed. Below is the structure of our leadership.

GOVERNANCE

Board of Directors

Our Board includes a significant representation of persons with disabilities. The Board provides strategic oversight, ensures accountability, and upholds our core values of dignity, inclusion, accountability, courage and justice.

LEADERSHIP

Executive Leadership

Day-to-day leadership of programmes, partnerships, advocacy and organisational development. Focused on centring lived experience in all decision-making and programme design.

PROGRAMMES

Programme Teams

Dedicated teams working across Advocacy & Policy Reform, Leadership & Empowerment, Inclusive Education & Livelihoods, Civic Participation, and Strategic Partnerships.

COMMUNITY

Grassroots Advocates & Volunteers

Persons with disabilities and allies who organise at community level, provide peer support, and ensure our work remains grounded in the realities of those we serve.
